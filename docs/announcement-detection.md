# First-lineup announcement detection

## Existing execution path

The native DB-due scheduler fetches configured official sources. Extraction and
policy evaluation persist an attempt and an identifier-only import event in one
transaction. The existing outbox and restricted receiver admit an agent only for
an actionable review/failure. Stable semantic issue keys, receipts and capability
leases suppress repeats; HTTP 409 is an expected claim conflict. Source backoff
and transport retries remain authoritative. No OpenClaw queue-watching job is
required, and successful ordinary checks do not invoke a model.

`announcementCoverageWarning` closes a silent date-only extraction gap. A strong
edition-bound announcement in visible headings, official links or accessible
poster captions, without extracted artists or an already recognized partial bill,
becomes a stable review warning. Navigation, scripts, footer/archive content,
coming-soon copy, off-origin links and other years do not establish an announcement.
The detector supplies no artist, date or ticket facts and cannot publish a lineup.
It is not a general web/social-media crawler or an OCR system. Missing explicit
edition/announcement evidence cannot be claimed as detected.

Sources for current editions still awaiting their first lineup can use daily
cadence. Production cadence is a separately audited source configuration change;
deployment alone does not change it. Existing failure backoff, leases and source
identity must be preserved. The initial rollout covers the existing empty-lineup
cohort; subsequently registered sources should follow the same daily policy until
their announcement parser is established.

## Graspop

The registered FAQ remains the canonical source. Its fetch now includes the
official English homepage, canonical artist list and at most sixteen linked news
cards. FAQ dates and each article's own edition/date statement bind the artist
list despite stale SEO/footer years. Each artist card must match a current news
card image, the article's canonical identity, named introductory statement,
explicit headline billing and correct performance day. Unknown/truncated/mixed
contracts require review; no names or billing are inferred from display order.

Verified paired additions use the normal fenced publisher with persisted
source/edition/evidence validation. Announcements are additive and partial:
missing cards do not delete saved artists or downgrade a complete record.
Reclassification still requires review. A first confirmed partial bill also
upgrades TBA record completeness inside that same transaction. An explicit
provider deferral prevents playlist jobs, including on replay. Festival ticket
sales are not inferred from train/parking purchases or a future onsale date.

The public fixtures are labelled canonical-link/main excerpts, not raw captures.
Full unchanged response discovery must also be checked during rollout. Production
acceptance requires a real native scheduler attempt and persisted/public result,
not just an enabled timer, a queued continuation or passing local fixtures.
